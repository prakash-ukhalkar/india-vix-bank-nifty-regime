=== TABLE 0 ===
Research gap | Risk if ignored | Design choice in this study
No India VIX regime analysis in prior Bank Nifty directional work | Pooled results may hide state-dependent predictability | Partition held-out test predictions into High-VIX and Low-VIX regimes using contemporaneous India VIX
Single-model claims dominate the literature | Apparent superiority may reflect model-specific inductive bias | Use a heterogeneous stacking ensemble with four tabular learners plus a BiLSTM sequence learner
Weak connection between prediction metrics and trading decisions | Statistically interesting results may be economically unusable | Translate the signal into a selective long-only deployment rule and compare against benchmark strategies
Limited robustness reporting around regime findings | Results may be threshold-specific or sample-fragile | Stress the result via threshold sensitivity, transaction costs, GARCH, bootstrap uncertainty, and cross-pipeline replication
