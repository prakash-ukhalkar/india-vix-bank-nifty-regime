## 2. Paper Summary

The paper proposes a **regime-aware expert decision system** for 21-day directional prediction of Bank Nifty (India's most actively traded equity index derivative).

**Core idea:**
- Train a stacking ensemble (XGBoost + LightGBM + Random Forest + CatBoost + BiLSTM with self-attention) on 16 leakage-safe technical indicators from 10 years of NSE daily data (2016-2026).
- Use **India VIX** (option-implied volatility) as a **reliability gate**: only deploy the directional model when VIX is elevated (High-VIX regime).
- The model achieves **84.5% directional accuracy** in High-VIX vs **54.8%** in Low-VIX on a 277-day held-out test (Jan 2025 - Mar 2026) — a 29.7 percentage-point difference.
- A selective long-only strategy (enter only when High-VIX + Up signal) achieves **Sharpe 2.22 vs 1.96** for buy-and-hold, with max drawdown **-3.69% vs -6.62%**, active only 48 of 277 test days.
- The effect is **horizon-specific**: no significant regime effect at 1-day or 5-day horizons.

## 5. All Three Reviewer Comments (Full Text)

### Reviewer 1 (7 comments)

**R1.1** — VIX threshold (15.08) computed as p75 of TEST period VIX distribution = look-ahead bias. Must be calibrated from training/validation data. A real-time system cannot know the test-period distribution in advance.

**R1.2** — 21-day prediction horizon causes consecutive observations to share 20 overlapping future days, inducing strong serial correlation. Fisher's exact test and bootstrap CIs assume independence — they are anti-conservative. Must use non-overlapping sampling, block bootstraps, or Newey-West/HAC correction.

**R1.3** — All 58 High-VIX test days cluster in one episode (2025 Q1 budget/US tariff shock). Cannot distinguish regime-general phenomenon from event-specific artifact. Cross-validation across independent High-VIX periods from different years required.

**R1.4** — **FACTUAL ERROR in manuscript:** Table 9 (Section 6.1) shows Sharpe drops to 1.936 at 50bps round-trip cost, which is BELOW buy-and-hold benchmark (1.963). But the text claims the strategy remains above the benchmark at every cost level. This must be corrected — the Sharpe advantage disappears at 50bps.

**R1.5** — Signal timing ambiguity: VIX and Bank Nifty closing prices are traded simultaneously. Must clarify: (a) exact minute signal is generated, (b) whether execution is at next-day open or same-day close, (c) how overlapping signals are managed.

**R1.6** — Test set unconditioned accuracy (61.01%) is below majority-class baseline (68.95%) due to bullish trend. To properly assess High-VIX accuracy of 84.5%, must report: majority-class baseline IN the High-VIX subset, plus precision, recall, F1 for High-VIX regime separately.

**R1.7** — Missing: (a) final hyperparameter sets from Optuna, (b) time-series CV fold structure, (c) chronological details of OOF probability generation. Also: wrong/irrelevant DOIs for Hamilton (1989) and Lundberg & Lee (2017).

---

### Reviewer 2 (6 comments)

**R2.1** — Same as R1.1: VIX threshold uses full test-window distribution (look-ahead bias). In real deployment, threshold must be known in advance.

**R2.2** — Same as R1.3: 58 High-VIX days concentrated in single sustained episode (2025 Q1-Q2). Bootstrap CIs assume independence, which is violated when High-VIX days cluster temporally.

**R2.3** — No comparison against simpler baselines under same VIX regime filter. Is the stacking ensemble's complexity justified? A majority-class predictor, persistence rule, or logistic regression might achieve similar performance in High-VIX regime.

**R2.4** — Sharpe ratio risk-free rate and annualization convention not stated. Cannot verify whether cross-strategy comparison is consistent.

**R2.5** — Claims volatility-sensitive features (Bollinger width, ATR-14, MACD dispersion) drive regime effect, but provides no SHAP values or feature importance rankings. SHAP analysis is described qualitatively only.

**R2.6** — Insufficient feature engineering details for reproducibility:
- MACD parameterization (12-26-9? EMAs up to t only?)
- Log-return lag convention: log(P_t/P_{t-k}) or log(P_{t-k+1}/P_{t-k})?
- Bollinger Band window length and MA type (simple or exponential?)
- Stochastic oscillator lookback (%K and %D, typically 14 days?)
- BiLSTM sequence overlap and potential leakage between train/val/test sequences
- Reviewer also recommends citing: GATE-GNN, EATSA-GNN, PIMPC-GNN (recent GNN papers)

---

### Reviewer 3 (6 comments)

**R3.1** — **LABEL LEAKAGE at split boundaries:** Observation near end of training period uses a future price inside the validation period to create its 21-day label. Same issue between validation and test. Must remove final 21 trading days before each split boundary and rerun analysis.

**R3.2** — Same as R1.1: VIX threshold computed from full test period (look-ahead bias).

**R3.3** — Same as R1.2: Statistical analysis must account for overlapping 21-day outcomes, especially as High-VIX observations are concentrated in one volatility episode.

**R3.4** — Ablation studies needed:
- Show contribution of each ensemble component separately
- Show contribution of BiLSTM self-attention specifically
- Provide hyperparameter table for reproducibility

**R3.5** — Several references are old. Literature review needs more recent studies on volatility-regime forecasting. References used sparingly — some claims need authoritative citations.

**R3.6** — Report regime-specific baselines and metrics. Clarify final target-realization date.

## 9. Key Technical Decisions Made

### Feature Engineering (documented for R2.6)
- MACD: EMA(12) - EMA(26), signal = EMA(9) of MACD; all EMAs use `adjust=False`
- RSI-14: Wilder smoothing via `ewm(com=13, adjust=False)`
- Stochastic %K: 14-day lookback; %D: 3-day SMA of %K
- Bollinger Bands: 20-day SMA (simple), 2 std
- ATR-14: Wilder smoothing `ewm(com=13, adjust=False)` of True Range
- Log-return lags: `log(Close[t] / Close[t-k])` -- backward looking, no leakage
- All features computed at time t using only data up to and including t

### VIX Threshold (fix for R1.1/R2.1/R3.2)
- PRIMARY: p75 of train+val VIX distribution (computed in NB-R02)
- Original biased value: 15.08 (from test period p75)
- New unbiased value: TBD (run NB-R02)

### Statistical Testing (fix for R1.2/R2.2/R3.3)
- Block bootstrap: circular, block_len=21, n_boot=10000
- Fisher's exact test on non-overlapping observations (every 21st day)
- Block permutation test (shuffle regime labels in blocks of 21)
- Bonferroni correction for 3 horizons tested (x3)

### Signal Execution Protocol (fix for R1.5)
- Signal generated AFTER close at 15:30 IST using Close[t] and VIX[t]
- Trade entry at NEXT DAY's open (t+1 open)
- Trade exit at Close[t+21]
- No same-day execution -- avoids look-ahead bias

### Sharpe Convention (fix for R2.4)
- Risk-free rate: 6.5% p.a. (India 91-day T-bill, RBI FY2025 avg)
- Daily RFR: 6.5% / 252
- Annualization: multiply by sqrt(252)
- Formula: Sharpe = (mean(daily_excess_return) / std(daily_excess_return)) * sqrt(252)

### 50bps Correction (fix for R1.4)
- At 50bps round-trip cost, strategy Sharpe FALLS BELOW buy-and-hold
- Original text erroneously claimed strategy stays above BH at all cost levels
- This must be corrected in manuscript text and in Table 9 caption
